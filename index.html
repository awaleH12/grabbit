A Download button on every video.

Add the Grabbit extension to Chrome or Edge and every video gets a Download button. Prefer links? Copy one anywhere and Grabbit fills it in by itself.

Works with YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, Instagram, X, WhatsApp statuses, Vimeo and many more.

  • TikTok with its real sound: Grabbit picks the copy with the video's own voice and music.
  • Facebook videos and Reels, including ones only your friends can see, using your browser's login.
  • WhatsApp statuses: open one in WhatsApp Web and press Save.

Pick the picture. The best sound comes with it.

Every copy a video has, from 360p to 4K, in H.264, VP9 or AV1, each with its size. Choose the picture and Grabbit adds the best sound, then joins them into one MP4.

  • Recommended is already picked: the best quality up to Full HD that plays everywhere.
  • For editing: tick one box and get an extra H.264 copy that Premiere Pro and CapCut open.
  • Small file picks a lighter copy, up to 480p, when data matters more than pixels.

Ask the AI why a moment works.

Choose Only a part and Grabbit downloads just those seconds. It marks the moments viewers replay most and the times people point at in the comments.

  • Explain with AI: Claude reads the video's captions and says what happens in each moment and why it would work as a short clip. It uses your own Claude key, usually a fraction of a cent per video.
  • Clip from here: in your browser, start a clip at the exact second you're watching.
  • Several clips from one video in one go, in original quality.

Made for data bundles.

Grabbit knows when your PC is on mobile data or a phone hotspot. It counts what it downloads there, stays out of your way on Wi-Fi, and stops before you go over your bundle.

  • Wait for Wi-Fi: on mobile data, a big file shows its size first, with a button to start it once you're back on Wi-Fi.
  • A monthly limit: enter your bundle and the day it renews. Grabbit warns at 80% and holds downloads at 100%.
  • Download tonight for night bundles. The PC can sleep or shut down when everything is done.

Windows says “Windows protected your PC”. Is Grabbit safe?

Windows shows this for new apps it hasn't seen many people download yet. Press More info, then Run anyway. The warning goes away as more people install Grabbit.

Is it free?

Yes, during launch: downloads, videos, clips, torrents, the data saver and Send to phone. When paid plans start, Grabbit tells you inside the app first and gives you a free trial. The AI explanations are billed to your own Claude account, usually a fraction of a cent per video.

Why does Grabbit ask for my phone number?

Your number is your Grabbit account. You enter it once to activate Grabbit, it lets you move Grabbit to a new PC, and it's the number you'll pay from with EVC Plus, Zaad or eDahab.

Which video sites work?

YouTube, TikTok, Facebook (videos and Reels), Instagram, X and many more. Some need you to be logged in: then use the Download button on the video in Chrome or Edge. Grabbit keeps its video support up to date by itself.

What happens when my internet drops?

The download waits, tries again by itself and carries on from where it stopped. If you close Grabbit or restart the PC, unfinished downloads continue next time.

Does the data counter include my browser?

No. It counts what Grabbit downloads on mobile data and hotspots. Wi-Fi isn't counted, and your browser and other apps use data too.
